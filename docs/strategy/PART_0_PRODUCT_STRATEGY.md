# Product Strategy

## Current Hackathon Scope

The hackathon does not retrieve, persist, cache, or display in-app market prices, historical charts, or financial metrics. A company `Price` action opens the supported ticker on Yahoo Finance in a separate tab. This temporary constraint does not change the longer-term product strategy.

## North Star

Become the best place on the internet for researching AI-related public companies.

## Strategic Positioning

AI Stocks Explorer is an AI-focused investment research platform that organizes public companies by their role in the AI value chain instead of only by traditional market sectors.

The product should help users understand:

* Which public companies matter to the AI ecosystem
* Why those companies matter
* How they are connected
* What financial and strategic signals are relevant to investors

## Target Users

Primary users:

* Individual investors
* Growth investors
* Technology-focused investors
* AI enthusiasts

Secondary users:

* Financial advisors
* Students
* Business analysts
* Technology professionals
* Product managers
* Venture capital researchers

## Differentiation

AI Stocks Explorer differs from traditional stock research platforms by combining:

* AI value chain organization
* Company relationship mapping
* Financial metrics
* AI-specific company context
* GPT-powered research assistance

## Product Principles

1. Investor-first clarity
2. AI explanations with guardrails
3. Relationship-driven discovery
4. Professional, trustworthy presentation
5. Scalable product and engineering foundation
